What We Offer

Our Massage Services

Each session is designed to help you feel more at ease — whether you're dealing with tension, soreness, or simply need a moment to breathe.

Full Body Massage

A gentle, flowing session that works through your whole body — from your shoulders and back to your legs and feet. Great for overall tension and stress relief.

30, 45, 60, 90, or 120 min

Deep Tissue Thai Massage

A deeper massage style that blends firm pressure with Thai-inspired stretching techniques to help release tight muscles, improve flexibility, and ease built-up tension.

30, 45, 60, 90, or 120 min

Foot Massage

A soothing session focused on your feet and lower legs — perfect after a long day of walking or standing. Simple, effective, and wonderfully relaxing.

60 or 90 min

Couples Massage

Unwind side by side with someone you care about. A lovely way to share a calming experience together without going too far from home.

30, 60, or 90 min

Chair Neck & Shoulder Massage

A focused chair massage for the neck, shoulders, upper back, and arms. Great for quick relief from desk work, driving, and everyday tension.

20 or 30 min
Special Add-Ons

Enhance Your Massage Experience

Make your massage even more relaxing with one of our special add-ons. These extra services are designed to help you unwind deeper, refresh your body, and enjoy a more personalized session.

🌸

Essential Oils

Add a calming aroma to your massage with essential oils. Their soft, natural scent helps create a peaceful atmosphere, making your session feel more soothing, refreshing, and relaxing from start to finish.

🌿

CBD Oil

Upgrade your massage with CBD oil for a deeper sense of comfort and relaxation. This add-on is a great choice for guests who want extra support for tired muscles, body tension, and everyday stress.

🫧

Cupping

Cupping uses gentle suction to help loosen tight areas and support muscle relaxation. It is a popular choice for guests who want focused care for stiffness, soreness, and built-up tension.

🔥

Hot Stone

Enjoy the warmth of smooth heated stones during your massage. Hot stones help melt away tension, calm the body, and add an extra layer of comfort to your relaxation experience.

✨

Body Scrub

Refresh your skin with a gentle body scrub that helps remove dry, dull skin and leaves you feeling soft, smooth, and renewed. It is a perfect add-on for a fresh, clean, spa-like feeling.

👣

Back Walking

Back walking uses controlled, balanced pressure to help stretch the muscles, ease stiffness, and relieve tension through the back and shoulders. It is ideal for guests who enjoy deeper pressure.
